# Maternity and Parental Leave Policy

## Maternity Leave
Employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children,
and 12 weeks for the third child onward, in line with statutory requirements. Leave may
begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

## Paternity Leave
Employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months
of the child's birth or adoption date.

## Adoption Leave
Employees who legally adopt a child below the age of 3 months are entitled to 12 weeks
of paid adoption leave.

## Return to Work
Employees returning from maternity leave are entitled to return to the same or an
equivalent role. Employees may request an additional period of unpaid leave of up to 4
weeks, subject to manager approval, before returning to work.

## Crèche Benefit
Employees with children under 6 years of age are eligible for a monthly crèche
reimbursement, subject to submission of valid receipts, for offices with fewer than 50
women employees where an on-site crèche is not provided.
