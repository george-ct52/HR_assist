# Work From Home (WFH) Policy

## Eligibility
All employees who have completed their probation period (90 days from date of joining)
are eligible to work from home, subject to manager approval and role suitability. Roles
requiring physical presence (e.g. facilities, front-desk) are excluded.

## Frequency
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week. Requests for more than 2 WFH days
in a week require written approval from the department head and HR.

## Core Hours
Employees working from home must be available and reachable during core hours,
10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, and must attend all scheduled meetings with video on
unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office setup allowance for employees
who WFH more than 3 days per week on a permanent basis. Employees are responsible for a
stable internet connection.

## Fully Remote Roles
Some roles are designated fully remote at the time of hiring. These employees are not
subject to the 2-day weekly cap but must still meet the core hours requirement.
