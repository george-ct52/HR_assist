# Medical Insurance Policy

## Coverage
All full-time employees are covered under the company's group medical insurance plan
from their date of joining. Coverage includes hospitalization, day-care procedures, and
pre/post hospitalization expenses for 30 and 60 days respectively.

## Sum Insured
The base plan covers up to INR 500,000 per employee per year. Employees can opt for a
top-up plan, at their own cost, to increase coverage up to INR 1,500,000.

## Dependents
Employees may add their spouse, up to two children, and dependent parents to the plan
during the annual enrollment window. Adding parents requires an additional premium
contribution from the employee, deducted from monthly payroll.

## Claims
Cashless claims are available at network hospitals. For non-network hospitals,
employees must pay upfront and submit a reimbursement claim with original bills within
30 days of discharge.

## Enrollment Changes
Outside the annual enrollment window, dependents can only be added or removed due to a
qualifying life event (marriage, birth of a child, loss of other coverage) within 30
days of the event.
